# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: May 8, 2026**

**ArmadilloMike.Dev** ("we," "us," or "our") operates the **Gork** Discord bot. This policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

## 1. Information We Collect
When you interact with Gork, we may collect the following information:
- **User IDs and Usernames:** To identify you and maintain your personalized experience.
- **Message Content:** We process the text of messages you send to Gork or in channels where Gork is active to generate AI responses.
- **User Memories:** Gork automatically extracts and stores facts you share (e.g., interests, names, preferences) to provide personalized interactions.
- **Usage Data:** We track interaction counts and the time of your last interaction.
- **Server/Channel IDs:** To manage configuration, whitelists, and blacklists.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data to:
- Provide and maintain the bot's functionality.
- Personalize your experience through stored "memories."
- Monitor the usage of our Service.
- Ensure compliance with our Terms of Service.

## 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties
Gork uses the **Hack Club AI** proxy to process AI requests.
- When you send a message to Gork, that content (and relevant context) is sent to Hack Club AI's servers for processing.
- We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
- We may disclose data if required by law or to protect our rights.

## 4. Data Retention
We retain user memories and usage data as long as necessary to provide the service. You may request the deletion of your stored memories by contacting a server manager with access to the bot's management commands or by contacting us directly.

## 5. Security
The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
